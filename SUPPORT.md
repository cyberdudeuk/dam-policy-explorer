# Getting help

| You want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Use the explorer | Open [cyberdudeuk.github.io/dam-policy-explorer](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/dam-policy-explorer/), or the Teams app if your department has added it. |
| Ask about the policy | Search the explorer's questions and answers first, then use the [contact form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/SLBvuPX7jK). |
| Report something that does not work | Open a [bug report](../../issues/new?template=bug.yml). |
| Report a security problem | Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not open a public issue. |

The GOV.UK pages on the Data Asset Management Policy are the authority. There is no telephone or email support.
