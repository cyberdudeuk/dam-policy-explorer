About the DAM Policy Explorer

A one-stop shop for the Data Asset Management Policy, made for DAM Policy coordinators and usable by anyone.

Why we made it

Coordinators told us the policy material was spread across GOV.UK, the Knowledge Hub, slide decks, recordings and email, and that some of it was hard to find or read. They asked for simpler, clearer answers in one place. This app brings it together and says where every answer comes from.

How we work

We follow the Service Standard (opens in a new tab), the GDS Way (opens in a new tab) and the GOV.UK guidance on writing (opens in a new tab). That means we start from users' needs, write in plain English, make every page work without JavaScript, test with assistive technology and publish what we change.

Where you can use it

  • Any web browser

    Current versions of Edge, Chrome, Safari and Firefox, on computers, tablets and phones. Pages work without JavaScript.

  • Microsoft Teams

    As a personal app in the Teams sidebar, on desktop, web and mobile. Its colours follow your Teams theme, including high contrast. You can also pin it in a channel as a website tab.

  • Outlook and the Microsoft 365 app

    The same app opens in Outlook and the Microsoft 365 app, once your administrator has made it available.

  • Installed, and offline

    Install it from your browser's menu, like an app. After your first visit, the pages you have opened work without a connection.

  • SharePoint and intranets

    Link to it, or embed a page using SharePoint's embed web part, where your organisation allows it.

  • Print and PDF

    Every page prints cleanly, with hidden sections opened and link addresses shown.

What we learned from similar services

We looked at how other governments and regulators help people put a data policy into practice, and used what works.

  • GOV.UK step-by-step navigation (opens in a new tab)The six-stage journey shown on every main page, and the twelve steps of the return.
  • GOV.UK question pages: one thing per page (opens in a new tab)The asset test asks one thing at a time and explains each result.
  • ICO data sharing information hub (opens in a new tab)Entry points by audience, myths and facts, checklists and templates in one place.
  • NSW Government Data Governance Toolkit (opens in a new tab)Guidance that works at any level of maturity, with a clear statement of what is required and what is advice.
  • Australian DATA Scheme: data sharing for data custodians (opens in a new tab)Step-by-step decisions that leave a record: the asset test's downloadable list of assets left out.
  • US Federal CDO Council implementation guidance (opens in a new tab)Written for the people who implement the policy, not from a policy view: plain answers, routes by role.

Transparency: how it was made

  • Sources. Facts about the policy come only from GOV.UK. Answers from GDS that are not yet published are labelled with the date they were given. Suggested practice is labelled as such.
  • Needs. The questions this app answers come from the GDS question register. Names and organisations are removed.
  • Contact form. Messages go to a Microsoft Form that only GDS can see. Your email address is asked for only if you want a reply, and is used only to reply.
  • Use of AI. Built with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic), which also drew the icons and illustrations. A person is responsible for the content.
  • Checks. Every build is tested for accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) at phone, tablet and desktop widths, in light, dark and high contrast, and for broken links and out-of-date wording.

Privacy

There are no cookies, no tracking and no analytics. Your display settings, your progress through the return and your list of tested assets are saved only in your own browser, on this device. Files you check are read by your browser and are never uploaded. To remove everything, choose Reset to standard in Display settings and clear the list on the asset test page.

Self-check

Each time it opens, the app checks itself and reports here.

The self-check needs JavaScript.

Updates

We re-read the GOV.UK sources and the question register at least every 30 days. If a page has not been checked for longer than that, it shows a warning at the top. Sources were last checked on 18 November 2026.

Roadmap

  • Now Publish agreed answers on asset boundaries, the legal gateway field and quality plan evidence as soon as they are agreed.
  • Now User research with coordinators on the explorer, including people who use assistive technology.
  • Next Host the explorer inside Teams for everyone in the coordinators' group.
  • Next Show each organisation's return status once returns arrive, with their agreement.
  • Later Welsh language version, and Easy Read summaries of the main pages.

What changed

Assets you are not sure about are now included provisionally and flagged. Contact by Microsoft Form. Version 3 of the return template confirmed.
  • 18 November 2026 Version 1.1.0. The explorer: home for coordinators, the policy in plain English, asset test, questions and answers built from forum questions, embedding in business as usual and change, resources, glossary with definitions on hover, display settings, and Teams theme support.
  • 13 November 2026 Version 1.0.0. Return steps, list check and the GDS team pages (earlier build, now part of the explorer).
